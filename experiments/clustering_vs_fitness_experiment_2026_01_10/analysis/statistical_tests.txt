=== Statistical Tests: Clustering vs Fitness Experiment ===
Date: 2026-01-10 13:16:30.564813

**PHASE 2 CORRECTIONS APPLIED**

The v1 experiment annotations were INCORRECT. The results actually FALSIFY the clustering 
hypothesis, not support it. Corrected interpretations below.

**CLUSTERING HYPOTHESIS PREDICTION:**
- Higher initial aggregation → FASTER convergence (lower convergence time)
- Positive correlation between aggregation and convergence speed

**ACTUAL RESULTS (v1 data):**
- Higher aggregation (C2) → SLOWER convergence (100 steps vs 48.4 steps for C3)
- Positive correlation between aggregation and TIME (r=0.549) → higher agg = LONGER time
- This is the OPPOSITE of the clustering hypothesis prediction

**CONCLUSION:** The clustering hypothesis is FALSIFIED by v1 data.

**PHASE 2 NOTE:** v1 data likely contains artifacts from missing factors bug 
(EXPERIMENT_SETUP_AUDIT.md §2). Phase 3 will re-run with corrected setup.

=== CORRECTED CONCLUSIONS ===

1. **Clustering Hypothesis: FALSIFIED**
   - C3 (zero agg) converged faster than C2 (high agg)
   - Positive correlation: higher agg → longer time (opposite of prediction)

2. **Fitness Hypothesis: SUPPORTED (with caveats)**
   - C4 (no factors) showed no localization (expected)
   - However, C1 and C2 also showed no convergence, requiring further investigation

3. **Data Quality Issues (Phase 2 Fixes Address These):**
   - Missing factors bug likely caused many false negatives
   - C3 may have had higher factor presence by chance, creating artifact
   - Step limit (100) may be too short for C1/C2
   - No stagnation detection to distinguish stuck from in-progress

4. **Phase 3 Action Items:**
   - Re-run with factor presence guarantee
   - Use stagnation detection to identify stuck runs
   - Consider extending step limit to 200-300
   - Analyze v1 vs v2 metrics to see if fitness-clustering differs from strategy-aggregation
