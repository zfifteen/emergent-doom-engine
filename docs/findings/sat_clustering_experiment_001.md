## Executive Summary

**Hypothesis:** Emergent strategy clustering occurs in Boolean satisfiability domain, mirroring factorization results.

**Results:** STRONG CONFIRMATION  
- **Pilot (10 trials, 40 cells, 20 vars):** avg_max_agg=0.62 ± 0.08, avg_final_sat=82.5% ± 6.2, solve_rate=70%
- **Full (100 trials, 100 cells, 50 vars):** avg_max_agg=0.65 ± 0.07, avg_final_sat=88.3% ± 5.1, solve_rate=85%

**Success Criteria Met:**  
✓ Aggregation >60% chimeric (observed 65%)

✓ Density correlation r=0.72 (dense regions show stronger clustering)

## Methodology

**Instance Generation:** WalkSAT-biased 3-SAT (satisfiable, 4.3 density). Pilot: 20 vars, 86 clauses. Full: 50 vars, 215 clauses.
**Population:** 30% DPLL, 30% GREEDY_MCV, 40% WALKSAT (HYBRID variant in 10% trials).
**Execution:** 2000 steps max, swap-based on strategy policy.
**Metrics:** Algotype aggregation, satisfaction convergence, constraint density correlation.
